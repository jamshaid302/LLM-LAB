# IT security rules

## Passwords

Company passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A password manager is provided and
must be used for every work account. Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; change one
only when you think it has leaked.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on every work account. Use the authenticator
app on your phone. Text message codes are not accepted as a second factor because they can
be stolen through SIM swapping.

## Laptops

Every company laptop has full-disk encryption turned on with BitLocker. The screen locks
automatically after 5 minutes without input. Never leave a laptop unattended in a car,
even in the trunk.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT service desk within 1 hour. The desk is
reached on internal extension 7000 at any time of day. IT will wipe the device remotely.

## Phishing

If an email looks suspicious, click the Report Phish button in the mail client. Do not
forward the email to coworkers. The security team reviews every report within one business
day.

## Networks and software

On public or hotel wifi, connect to NorthVPN before opening any work system. New software
is requested through the Software Catalog; installing software from other sources is not
allowed on company laptops.
