# Leave policy

## Vacation

Every full-time employee receives 25 vacation days per calendar year. Vacation is requested
in the Leave Tool at least two weeks before the first day off, and the request is approved
by your direct manager. Part-time employees receive vacation in proportion to their hours.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried into the next year. Carried days must be used
by March 31, and any carried days left after that date are lost. Days beyond the 5-day
limit are not paid out unless local law requires it.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Tell your manager before your normal start
time on the first day you are sick. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick
days. Sick days do not carry over into the next year.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave can start up to two weeks
before the expected birth or adoption date and must be taken within the first year.

## Public holidays

The company observes 11 public holidays per year. The list for each country is published
in the Leave Tool every December. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, the next working
day is taken off instead.

## Unpaid leave and sabbatical

After 5 years of service an employee can request a sabbatical of up to 3 months of unpaid
leave. The request must be made at least 3 months in advance and approved by both the
manager and the people team.
