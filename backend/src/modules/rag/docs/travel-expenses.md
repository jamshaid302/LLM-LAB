# Travel and expenses

## Booking

All business trips are booked through TripDesk, the company travel portal. A trip must be
booked at least 14 days before departure unless the manager approves a late booking.
Bookings made outside TripDesk are not reimbursed.

## Flights

Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or longer can
be booked in business class, but only with written approval from a director. Seat upgrades
paid by the employee are allowed and are not reimbursed.

## Hotels

The hotel limit is 180 USD per night in most cities. In high-cost cities such as London,
New York, and Tokyo, the limit is 260 USD per night. Rooms above the limit need manager
approval before the stay.

## Meals and per diem

The daily meal allowance, called the per diem, is 45 USD for each full travel day. On the
day you leave and the day you return, the per diem is half, so 22.50 USD. Alcohol is never
reimbursed.

## Receipts

Keep an itemized receipt for any expense above 25 USD. Expenses without a receipt above
that amount are rejected. Photos of receipts uploaded to TripDesk count as receipts.

## Mileage and reimbursement

Driving a personal car on business is reimbursed at 0.40 USD per kilometer. Approved
expense claims are paid to your bank account within 10 business days of approval.
