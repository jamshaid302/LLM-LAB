# First weeks for new employees

## First day

On your first day, arrive at the reception desk at 9:30. Reception gives you a visitor
badge for the morning and walks you to your team. Your permanent badge is printed before
lunch.

## Laptop pickup

Pick up your laptop from the IT bar on the 2nd floor on your first day. IT sets up your
password manager and your authenticator app with you at the desk, which takes about 30
minutes.

## Onboarding buddy

Every new employee is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for the first 30
days. The buddy answers everyday questions and has lunch with you in the first week.

## Training

The security awareness course must be completed within 7 days of your start date. The
code of conduct course must be completed within 30 days. Both courses are in the Learning
Hub.

## Probation

The probation period lasts 90 days. During probation you meet your manager one-to-one
every week. At the end of probation the manager writes a short review with you.
