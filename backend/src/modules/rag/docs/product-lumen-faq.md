# Lumen Notes product FAQ

## What is Lumen Notes?

Lumen Notes is the note-taking app our company sells. Notes are written in Markdown, can be
shared with teammates, and sync across the web, desktop, and mobile apps.

## Plans and prices

The Free plan includes up to 3 notebooks and 1 GB of storage. The Pro plan costs 8 USD per
month and includes unlimited notebooks and 100 GB of storage. The Team plan costs 15 USD
per user per month and adds single sign-on (SSO) and an admin console.

## Refunds

Paid plans can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, a cancelled plan
stays active until the end of the billing period and is not refunded.

## Uptime

The Team plan includes an uptime service level agreement (SLA) of 99.9 percent per month.
If uptime falls below that, Team customers receive a 10 percent credit on the next invoice.
Free and Pro plans have no SLA.

## Data location

Customers choose where their data is stored when they create a workspace. The two regions
are the EU (Frankfurt) and the US (Virginia). The region cannot be changed after the
workspace is created.

## Export

Notes can be exported as Markdown or PDF at any time, on every plan. A full workspace
export is a zip file emailed to the workspace owner.

## API

The Lumen API is available on the Pro and Team plans. It allows 600 requests per minute per
workspace. Requests above that limit receive HTTP 429 and should be retried after the
time in the Retry-After header.
